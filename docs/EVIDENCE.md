# Evidence and attribution

The event challenge is motivated by one documentary finding summarized from the **October 9 Access sample/reference v0.4**: daily care, tutoring and drop-in enrichment are different service types and should be compared with a family's actual schedule and care needs. [Research reference](https://app.notion.com/p/3f4864a4a0b3816da66bda4e08d4b5de) (access may be restricted).

This attribution is a concise research-context statement. The repository does not reproduce the private reference, participant records, provider records or unpublished tables. Attendees can complete the challenge using the repository alone. Do not ask them to retrieve the private page or upload it to a model.

The final 21-page RC2 brief was not readable during the November 2 preparation run (Library access returned 403 in the research workflow). Exact correspondence to RC2 has not been established. Do not label this challenge a validated implementation of that final brief.

All excerpts, normalized facts, fictional needs and reference labels under [data/care-fit](../data/care-fit/README.md) were newly authored for this exercise. They illustrate the distinction; they are not empirical Access Lab data, actual Durham services or evidence of outcomes. The older [fixtures](../fixtures/) retain their original synthetic status.

Reference labels were authored separately from the deterministic baseline and checked for internal consistency during preparation. This is technical review, not domain approval. The event domain reviewer remains **to be assigned**. Before presenting a reviewed challenge, record reviewer, date, source version, changes and unresolved disagreements in the [review checklist](REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md). Preserve prior labels and the reason for a correction in Git history.

The full research reference also motivates future work on cost units, program-specific aid, handoffs, application stages and source conflicts. This event implements one bounded service/schedule comparison; it makes no claim to solve those broader questions. All seats, admission, eligibility and outcome claims remain unknown.
